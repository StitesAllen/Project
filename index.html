About Me

Hi, I’m Samuel Stites Allen, a senior studying engineering at the University of Vermont. I’m pursuing a bachelor’s degree through UVM’s College of Engineering and Mathematical Sciences, with a focus split primarily between Civil and Mechanical Engineering. Over the past four years, I’ve worked on a variety of engineering projects, both independently and as part of a team. These projects include but are not limited to structural analyses and finite element analyses, check out some these projects on my Projects tab.

I'm originally from Louisville, Kentucky, where I was born and raised. Outside of class, I’m a passionate golfer — I play on UVM's club golf team and am always on the lookout for new courses to explore. But golf is just one part of my athletic life. I’ve been playing soccer since I was two and continued through high school. These days, I still make time for intramural soccer, along with a long list of other sports. I play intramural broomball (a wild, skate-free version of hockey on ice), basketball, volleyball, pickleball, wiffleball, trenchball (dodgeball), and even some ice hockey.

I’m also a self-proclaimed nerd, and I don’t limit myself to just one realm of nerddom. I’m a huge Star Wars fan — complete with a couple of lightsabers and an extensive LEGO Star Wars collection. I’m into a wide range of video games across different genres, and to a slightly lesser degree, I’ve picked up a fair amount of superhero knowledge as well. One of my favorite hobbies is graphic design. I grew up in a home that deeply valued art, and graphic design became my creative outlet into that world. Check out my portfolio to see the drawings I’m most proud of!
